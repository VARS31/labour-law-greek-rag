# Chunking report: labour law (Phase 1A)

How the 1,284 labour-law documents (volume 41, *ΕΡΓΑΤΙΚΗ ΝΟΜΟΘΕΣΙΑ*) were cleaned and cut into chunks for search. This file is generated by [`scripts/build_chunks.py`](../scripts/build_chunks.py) from the real output in `data/processed/labour_chunks.jsonl`; every number and example below comes from that run. No AI, no embeddings, no database were used.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Documents | 1,284 |
| **Chunks** | **5,740** (Phase 0 estimated about 5,301) |
| Words per chunk | median 81, average 133, shortest 11, longest 500 |
| Chunking method | by article: 445 documents; by numbered paragraph: 140; by size: 13; kept whole (short): 686 |
| Page noise removed | 19,368 words out of 769,005 (2.5%), from 615 documents |
| Article headings moved to their own article | 1,222 |
| Stubs (document under 30 words) | 177 documents |
| Editorial notes | 185 chunks |
| Repeal sentences kept | 256 chunks in 173 documents |

Why 5,740 and not 5,301: the Phase 0 estimate had no overlap and no paragraph fallback. Here, long pieces are cut into windows that share about 50 words (so they need a few more chunks), 140 documents without articles are split by numbered paragraph instead of being one big piece, and the article regex no longer counts references like "Άρθρο 3 της οδηγίας" as markers.

## What happens to each document

1. **Normalise** the characters (same module will clean user questions later).
2. **Remove printed-page noise**: page numbers, update-issue lines, book position codes and the running titles printed next to them.
3. **Read the header**: act type, number, year, ΦΕΚ and title.
4. **Cut into pieces**: by article if there are article markers, otherwise by numbered paragraph, otherwise by size. The header/preamble is its own piece. In the editors' style an article's heading comes *before* its marker ("… οδηγεί η κλίμαξ. Φορηταί κλίμακες Άρθρ.17.-…"), so after cutting, a heading left at the end of a piece is moved to the start of the next one.
5. **Tidy the pieces**: pieces under 20 words join the previous one; pieces over 500 words are cut into windows of at most 500 words, sharing about 50 words with the next window, so a sentence on the border is never lost.
6. **Label each chunk** and build the *text to embed*: a short header ("Ν. 2994/1922, Άρθρο 9, title…") plus the chunk text.

## 2. Printed-page noise

The texts come from a loose-leaf book, and its page headers ended up inside sentences. A header block mixes *anchors* with a fixed shape (always removed) and *running titles*, which are ordinary Greek words. Running titles are removed only when they are on a list learned from the data and touch an anchor; words that are not on the list are never removed.

| Part of a header block | Removed |
|---|---:|
| Replacement notes `(Αντί για τη σελ. 91(θ)`, `(Μετά τη σελ. …)` | 671 |
| Page numbers `Σελ. 228`, `Σελίς 307`, `Σελ. 30,207(α)` | 1,421 |
| Update issues `Τεύχος 1294-Σελ. 33` | 1,099 |
| Book position codes `15.Λ.α.58`, `15.Γ .β.20` | 1,459 |
| Running titles | 1,447 |
| Bare page numbers next to them (`451`, `- 151`, `ΙΣΤ`) | 776 |
| **Header blocks in total** | **1,959** in 615 documents |
| Words removed | 19,368 (2.5% of the text) |
| Words cut in two by a page break and joined again (`υπαλ` + `ληλικής`) | 83 |

**The title list.** 50 running titles were learned from text that sits *between* two anchors and appears at least twice (so a stray sentence can't get in). The 42 chapter and subject names that were not already on the list are used too, but more carefully: only when more header follows them. ## 4. Labels on each chunk

Each line of `labour_chunks.jsonl` is one chunk with these fields:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `id` | stable id `glc-<document>-<sequence>`, e.g. `glc-03244-002` |
| `doc_id, split` | document number (0-based, train → validation → test, as in Phase 0) and its split |
| `volume, chapter, subject` | the dataset labels (names) |
| `act_type, act_abbr, act_number, year, fek` | from the header, when found |
| `title` | title of the act, from the header |
| `citation` | how the chunk names its source: `Ν. 2994/1922`, or, without an act number, act type + «title» + (chapter, year) |
| `citation_fallback` | `true` when the second form was needed |
| `method, kind` | how the document was cut, and what this chunk is (preamble / article / …) |
| `article, article_numbers` | label like `Άρθρο 5` or `Άρθρα 13-19`, and the numbers as integers |
| `part, parts` | window number when a long piece was cut, e.g. 2 of 3 |
| `words` | length of the chunk text |
| `is_stub` | the whole document is under 30 words |
| `is_note` | the chunk is mainly an editorial note, not law text: notes (brackets, or sentences starting with e.g. `Κατηργήθη`, `Τροποποιηθείς`) cover at least half of it |
| `repeal_sentences` | the short sentence around each past-tense repeal verb, e.g. `Κατηργήθη δια του άρθρ. 12 Α.Ν. 1836/1951 κατωτ. αριθ. 15.`, kept for later |
| `text` | the cleaned chunk text |
| `embed_text` | short header + newline + text; this is what will be embedded |

How often the header gave us each field (per document):

| Act type | Act number | Year | ΦΕΚ | Title |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 99% | 85% | 99% | 69% | 100% |

Missing numbers are mostly real: many royal decrees and old laws have no number in the header ("ΒΑΣΙΛΙΚΟΝ ΔΙΑΤΑΓΜΑ της 7/9 Φεβρ. 1951 …"), and old acts have no ΦΕΚ reference.

## 7. Fixes made at the start of Phase 1B

Three rules were tightened before loading the chunks into the database. The *before* numbers come from the Phase 1A rules, run again on the same chunks.

### Every chunk names its source

1,113 of 5,740 chunks come from documents without an act number in the header (106 of them without even an act type). Their header used to start with just the article label, so the embedded text didn't say where it came from. Now it uses a fallback: act type (if known), the title in «», and the chapter and year in brackets. The same text is stored in the new `citation` field, and `citation_fallback` is `true` for these chunks.

### `is_note` only when the note is the main content

The old rule flagged a chunk when its text *started* with a bracket or an editorial verb. That also caught real law text that only opens with a short source reference ("Άρθρον 18 (άρθρ. 18 Δ. 23 Νοεμ. 1928, άρθρ. 7 ν. 4388). 1.Κατά τας Κυριακάς …"), and chunks where the source lost a closing bracket. Now the notes at the start of a chunk are measured, and the chunk is flagged only when they cover at least 50% of it. A bracket that doesn't close within 60 words is treated as lost and counts only up to the end of its first sentence.

### Repeal sentences: only the short sentence around the verb

The old rule kept the whole "sentence" around a repeal verb, but sentence ends are rare in this text, so it often grabbed a long, unrelated stretch. Now it keeps a short window: from the start of the sentence, a colon, a list item (`γ)`) or an open bracket, at most 15 words before the verb, to the end of the sentence or the closing bracket, at most 25 words after it. Abbreviations such as "Υπουργ." no longer count as sentence ends.

| | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Sentences kept | 275 | 282 |
| Chunks with at least one | 256 | 256 |
| Words per sentence: median / 90% / longest | 23 / 60 / 69 | 18 / 33 / 41 |

## Known limits of this step

- Running titles that appear only once and are not chapter or subject names stay in the text. That is the safe side: a few extra words instead of lost law text.
- A word cut by a page break is joined only when the break sits right against it; a few such words may remain split.
- Article order inside a document is kept as found (often shuffled); use `article_numbers`.
- Tables, formulas and forms survive badly (see the worst cases); they are kept, not repaired.
- A title ends at the first full stop after a word of 5+ letters. Short abbreviations ("Υπ.", "Εθν.") don't end it, but a longer one ("Κοινων.") still can.
- A moved heading is recognised only as an unfinished phrase (no full stop) of at most 15 words at the end of a piece; a heading that contains an abbreviation with a full stop stays where it is.
- `is_note` recognises editorial sentences by how they start (a bracket or a verb such as "Κατηργήθη"). An editorial sentence that starts differently ("Ο δια του ανωτέρω Β.Δ. … συσταθείς …") doesn't count, so a few chunks that are mostly notes stay unflagged. That errs on the side of keeping law text visible.
